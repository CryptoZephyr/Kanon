# Kanon Demo Boundary

Canonical source: [Kanon Demo in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/3cac5381831281eeb045dd0f2f71efe4).

The verified proof uses one representative business financial agent. It shows company-defined terms, exact human approval, a restricted Privy signer and policy, a real ENSv2 identity with protected approved-state records, one allowed action, one rejected out-of-policy action, an authority-expanding release blocked pending human decision, ordered Privy then ENS synchronization, revoke, revoked ENS status, and failed post-revoke execution.

The proof is limited to the configured Ethereum Sepolia testnet namespace. Evidence is recorded in [evidence/deployment/t17-live-latest.json](evidence/deployment/t17-live-latest.json) and [evidence/lifecycle/t11-t13-latest.json](evidence/lifecycle/t11-t13-latest.json).
