# Kanon Demo Boundary

Canonical source: [Kanon Demo in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/3cac5381831281eeb045dd0f2f71efe4).

The eventual proof is one representative business financial agent. It must show company-defined terms, exact human approval, restricted Privy signer and policy, a real ENSv2 identity with protected approved-state records, one allowed action, one rejected out-of-policy action, an authority-expanding release blocked pending human decision, ordered Privy then ENS synchronization, revoke, revoked ENS status, and failed post-revoke execution.

Until those live controls exist, no sponsor behavior is claimed by this repository.
