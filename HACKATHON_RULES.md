# Kanon Hackathon Scope

Canonical source: [Kanon Hackathon Rules in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/3cac5381831281098581dfb330536587).

Kanon is a Start Fresh project for ETHOnline 2026 targeting the Privy B2B financial product, Privy financial flow, and ENS Best Use of ENSv2 tracks. It does not target the ENS continuity prize because it had no pre-event source code.

The two sponsor integrations are structural. Claims must stay limited to verified Privy policy-enforced wallet authority and verified ENSv2 company-controlled identity, namespace, and scoped record permissions. The build does not claim production custody readiness, ENSv2 financial enforcement, or mainnet stability for beta Sepolia interfaces.
